## What does it mean to dream about your stomach?

The stomach is the body's processing centre — not just for food, but in dream symbolism for experiences and emotions. "Gut feeling" isn't just a figure of speech — we often sense something in the stomach before the mind has put it into words. Dreams about the stomach speak to this intuitive, body-based form of wisdom. It's a different kind of body dream from dreams about the [heart](/en/dreams/heart/), which tend to concern love and courage.

## Gut feeling and what you digest

### Intuition and gut feeling

The most common and most interesting interpretation concerns what you know without having consciously thought it — the subconscious signal that sits lower in the body than reason does. Dreaming about your stomach can be the subconscious's way of saying: listen to your gut. It knows something your mind isn't ready to admit.

### Digesting difficulty

The stomach digests — and in dreams this can mean you're processing something difficult from your life. A situation you've swallowed without working through it, an experience that still needs more time to "settle." The stomach symbol in a dream is a reminder that processing takes time.

### Emotional unease in the body

Anxiety and unease tend to settle in the stomach — we feel butterflies, cramping, nausea. Dreaming about your stomach can mirror an emotional state so strong it has taken up residence in the body. What is it you're dreading, afraid of, or feeling deep down?

## What you felt in your stomach

**If your stomach was in pain:**
Something you've swallowed is hard to digest. Something feels wrong.

**If you were pregnant in the dream (stomach):**
See the symbol page for [pregnant](/en/dreams/pregnant/) — something new is growing.

**If your stomach was empty:**
A basic need that isn't being met — care, nourishment, meaning.

**If something was wrong inside your stomach:**
Something hidden is affecting you — an unease, a fear, a truth you're carrying.

**If your stomach was strong and healthy:**
Good health, strong intuition, and the ability to process what life brings you. Dreams about food and nourishment touch on similar territory.

## Gut feeling, intuition and what we digest

Dreaming about your stomach can be about intuition — the certainty you feel before you've managed to put it into words. "Trust your gut" isn't just a saying: researchers call the enteric nervous system in the gut the body's "second brain," and it constantly sends signals to the brain about how you're really doing. Dreams about the stomach can be a reminder to listen to what you sense, before reason has had a chance to override it.

The stomach is also where we "digest" what happens to us. "Butterflies in the stomach" or not being able to "stomach" something are images of how experiences and feelings get processed in the body. A dream about your stomach can therefore mirror something you're still working through — an experience that needs more time to settle.

The stomach can also be about nourishment and need — hunger, what actually gives you energy, or a vulnerability you carry (the soft, unprotected stomach). Sometimes the explanation is simpler than you'd think: actual hunger or digestion while you sleep can also shape your dream imagery, and that's nothing to worry about.

## Sources and further reading

- Artemidorus of Daldis, *Oneirocritica* (c. 150–200 AD) — the body parts in dreams, with the stomach as the seat of what you digest.
- G. William Domhoff, *The Scientific Study of Dreams* (2003) — the continuity hypothesis: the gut feeling in the dream is often the one you have while awake.
