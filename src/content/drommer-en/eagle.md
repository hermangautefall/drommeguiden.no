## What does it mean to dream about an eagle?

No other animal sees the world from quite as high up as the eagle, and in nearly every culture it carries a message about power, freedom, and altitude. It sees what others can't. It moves where others can't reach — a quality it shares with dreams about [flying](/en/dreams/flying/). Dreaming about an eagle is rarely a small dream.

The interpretation depends a great deal on what the eagle was doing, and what feeling it stirred in you.

## Where the air gets thin

### Perspective and overview

The eagle's defining trait is its eyesight — sharp and wide-ranging from a great height. In dreams, the eagle is often about exactly this: the need for, or the ability to, rise above what's close and see the whole picture. You may have dreamed of an eagle during a period when you're too close to a situation and need distance to understand it.

The dream can be encouraging you to take a step back and see the bigger picture.

### Freedom and independence

The eagle can't be tamed. In dreams, it can symbolise a wish for — or a movement toward — greater freedom in your life. Maybe you're in a situation that feels restrictive, and the dream mirrors a deep desire to break out and follow your own course.

### Ambition and high goals

In many traditions, the eagle is a symbol of rulers and leaders — the one who governs from the top. Dreaming about an eagle can mean that ambitions you've carried for a long time are close to being realised, or that you're ready to take on more responsibility.

## Soaring, diving, or caught

If the eagle flew high and free in the dream, that's a strong positive sign — freedom, overview, and a sense of mastery are within reach. If it dived suddenly toward the ground, the dream is often about focus and concentration: you're ready to seize something you've had your eye on for a while. If the eagle attacked you, it can point to someone with real authority or power pressing on you — or to your own ambitions starting to feel threatening in their scale. If the eagle was injured or trapped, it often mirrors a feeling that your freedom or your options are limited right now, and that something is holding you back.

## The view from the top of the tree

In Norse mythology, an eagle sat at the very top of Yggdrasil, the world tree, high above the [mountains](/en/dreams/mountain/) — the highest of all creatures, holding wisdom and a view over the whole world. Dreaming about an eagle in that tradition was read as a sign of divine attention or a significant fate.

Jung saw birds in general as symbols of liberated thought and the soul in free movement. The eagle in particular represents the highest level of consciousness — the ability to see yourself and your life with clarity and distance. If you keep dreaming about an eagle, it may be worth noticing what kind of height or distance you're seeking in your life right now.
