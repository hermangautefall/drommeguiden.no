## What does it mean to dream about your ears?

In dream symbolism, the ear is the organ of listening — and so it becomes a symbol of attention, communication, and opening yourself to something beyond yourself. Dreams about ears are, in the broadest sense, about hearing: what are you listening to, what are you shutting out, and what's trying to reach you? They sit close to dreams about [eyes](/en/dreams/eyes/), which are about what you see and don't see.

## Hearing, and being heard

### Something is trying to reach you — listen more closely

The most common reading involves a message you haven't picked up on — something someone is trying to tell you, something in your surroundings you should be noticing, or something from your own inner voice you've been overlooking. The dream about your ear is a direct nudge: pay more attention. Listen.

### A wish to be heard

Dreams about ears can be just as much about being heard as about hearing others. A wish for someone to genuinely listen, to understand you, to take you seriously. A period where you feel like what you're saying isn't landing.

### What you choose to let in

Ears can be closed — we can choose what we listen to, what we believe, what we let through. Dreams about ears can, at times, be about exactly this choice: what are you letting into your awareness, and what are you shutting out?

## What you picked up, or didn't

**If your ears were large:**
A strong capacity for listening and openness — you're taking in a lot from the world around you.

**If your ears were blocked:**
Something is stopping you from hearing — either outside noise, or an inner resistance to letting something in.

**If your ears were injured:**
A communication breakdown — something in your connection to the world around you has been damaged.

**If you heard something specific in the dream:**
What you heard is loaded with meaning — what did they say?

**If you couldn't hear at all:**
A kind of isolation or a cut-off from something important. See also the [mouth](/en/dreams/mouth/), which is about what you express.

## Listening as a form of wisdom

In many spiritual traditions, the ability to listen is tied to wisdom — the wise are said to listen more than they speak. To dream about your ears is to dream about the basic foundation of understanding: whether what's being sent is actually being received.

A blocked-ear dream can also be read as a sign that some part of you doesn't want to hear something from the unconscious — a resistance to insight that needs to be worked through. Dreams about [strangers](/en/dreams/stranger/) can carry a related theme: listening to something unfamiliar within yourself.

## Sources and further reading

- Artemidorus of Daldis, *Oneirocritica* (c. 150–200 AD) — the body's parts in dreams, and the ear tied to what's heard and what's overheard.
- G. William Domhoff, *The Scientific Study of Dreams* (2003) — the continuity hypothesis: what you don't hear in the dream is often something you're avoiding.
