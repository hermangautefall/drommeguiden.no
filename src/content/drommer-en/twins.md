## What does it mean to dream about twins?

Twins are, in themselves, one of nature's small mysteries — two separate beings arising from one. In the dream's visual language, they're one of the purest symbols of inner duality: something that is two and one at the same time.

## Two sides of the same self

### Two sides of yourself, in conflict or coexisting

The central reading: something in you is doubled. Two wants pulling in different directions. Two sides of your identity that are both real, but not always easy to hold at once.

Maybe you're pulled between career and family life. Between two sets of values. Between who you are and who you think others expect you to be. The twins are these two sides — identical in origin, but grown in different directions.

The question the dream is asking isn't which one is right. It's whether you can make room for both.

### An expectation that turned out to be double

Sometimes the twin dream is simpler: you expected one thing and got two. A plan that turned out more complicated than intended. A project that demanded twice what you'd budgeted for. A surprise — not necessarily a bad one, but bigger than expected.

### The persona and the shadow — a doubled self

Jung described the human psyche as fundamentally double: the social persona (the mask we show the world) and the shadow (what we suppress and hide away). Twins in a dream can represent exactly this pair — who you are outwardly and who you are inwardly, the accepted and the disowned.

Dreaming of twins who look different from each other can reflect a growing awareness: both sides are you, and both need room to exist.

### Creative work that outgrows the plan

For anyone in a creative or generative phase, twins can symbolise that what's developing is richer and more complex than planned — two ideas instead of one, two possibilities growing in parallel.

## Whether they were identical or different

**If the twins were identical:**
Two sides that are hard to tell apart — something in you is split but not opposed. What's the double here?

**If the twins were clearly different:**
Two distinct, maybe even opposite, sides. What did each of them represent?

**If you were the twin:**
You're experiencing yourself as double — who are you, really, and who is "the other you"?

**If one twin was ill or weak:**
One side of you needs care. The strong side and the fragile side of something exist side by side.

**If you felt happy about both:**
A positive sign of capacity — you can hold the complexity of who you are.

## The double in psychology and myth

Twins carry a distinct mythological charge across nearly every culture. Romulus and Remus, the founders of Rome, in which one killed the other. Castor and Pollux, one mortal and one divine, and yet inseparable. Horus and Set in Egyptian mythology — order and chaos as twinned forces.

The archetypal twin motif is, throughout, about doubleness as a necessary tension — not one truth, but two that are both true at once. Jung held that the self is always double: conscious and unconscious, light and shadow, known and unfamiliar. The twin dream is the psyche's most direct image of that doubleness — proof that holding two things at once isn't a contradiction to resolve, but a fact of being a whole person.

## Sources and further reading

- G. William Domhoff, *The Scientific Study of Dreams* (2003) — the continuity hypothesis, and why sameness or difference in the dream mirrors an inner tension.
