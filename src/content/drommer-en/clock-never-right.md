## What does it mean when the clock is never right in a dream?

You look at the clock. It says quarter past seven. You look away for a moment,
look back, and now it says half ten. Or the hands are missing. Or the numbers are
there but will not resolve, and the harder you try, the less sense they make.

The same happens with text. A line of letters you saw clearly a second ago is
something else when you look again. The phenomenon is so widespread that it has
acquired a practical use.

## Numbers and letters in dreams

### Writing that will not hold still

Written language is something we learned rather than something we are born with,
and reading takes a fairly specialised effort. One possible reading is that the
dream does not have that effort available, and therefore produces something
resembling text without being it.

### The clock as more than numbers

A clock is not just a display. It is our entire way of organising a day, and that
makes it a rewarding image. A clock that is wrong can mirror a period when time
does not feel like your own.

### The reality check

Because text and numbers are so unstable, they are used by people practising
becoming aware that they are dreaming. You look at the clock, look away, and look
back. If it has changed, you are not awake. The same goes for light switches,
which rarely work as they should.

## What the clock did

**If time jumped forward:**
Sits close to dreams of [being late](/en/dreams/being-late/), and tends to come in
periods with pressure to make something in time.

**If the clock ran backwards:**
Often described as less stressful than the first variant, and can mirror a wish to
do something over.

**If the numbers could not be read at all:**
The purest form of the phenomenon, and the one that most often leads people to
realise they are dreaming.

**If you looked at your phone instead:**
The [phone](/en/dreams/phone/) in dreams is often even less reliable than the
clock. Screens that cannot be read or typed on belong to the same family.

**If you lost track entirely:**
Can be read in the direction of having [lost something](/en/dreams/losing-something/)
you thought you had control of, rather than about time as such.

## When the dream comes in busy periods

Clock dreams tend to present themselves when the calendar is tight, when something
has to be made in time, and when more people than you have a claim on your hours.
It is one of the dreams that translates fairly directly.

If you notice you check the clock often in dreams, it is worth writing down. For
many it is the first sign of being closer to lucidity than they think.

  periods when time is short while awake.
