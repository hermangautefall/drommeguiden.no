## What does it mean to dream of a tsunami?

The horizon is wrong. It takes a moment to understand what you are looking at, and by the
time you do it is already too late to get away. Many people wake in that very second.

The dream is among the most intense water dreams there is, and it differs from
[flooding](/en/dreams/flood/) on one decisive point. A flood rises gradually and gives you
time. A tsunami does not.

## The catastrophe you watch arrive

### Everything at once

Where most dreams of being overwhelmed concern something building up, this one concerns
something arriving all together. It tends to come in periods when several things could go
wrong at the same time, or when a single decision changes everything.

### The time that is not enough

The characteristic feature is the mismatch between seeing and being able to act. You are told
what is happening, but not in time. That is a precise image for situations where you
understand something before you can influence it.

### Why the dream is so visual

People remember tsunami dreams unusually well and describe them in images rather than
feelings. That is probably because the threat is visible and bounded, unlike most other
nightmare motifs.

## What you did in the dream

**If you ran uphill:**
The most common reaction. Seeking height is instinctive, and the dream usually follows
instinct further than logic.

**If you tried to warn someone:**
Then responsibility is the theme, not danger. Who you tried to reach is the most important
information the dream gives.

**If you simply stood still:**
Common, and not a sign of passivity while awake. Many describe a calm that surprised them.

**If the wave never hit:**
A frequent variant. The dream often stops just before, and that says more about waking than
about the content.

**If the water drew back first:**
The real warning sign, and an image many recognise: something becomes unusually quiet just
before it happens.

## When the dream comes in pressured phases

The dream tends to arrive in periods of accumulated strain — when several things have been
unresolved for a long time and something is about to give. It is less often an expression of
one particular fear than of the sum of them.

The dream sits close to [waves](/en/dreams/wave/), but there the event is something you can
come through. Here it is total.

If the dream or this article brings up strong feelings, talk to someone you trust.

## Sources and further reading

- Antti Revonsuo, "The reinterpretation of dreams" (2000) — threat simulation theory, relevant
  because a visible, unavoidable catastrophe is the model situation the theory describes.
- Ernest Hartmann, *The Nature and Functions of Dreaming* (2011) — on nightmares and emotional
  processing, relevant to why the dream is remembered so visually.
- G. William Domhoff, *The Scientific Study of Dreams* (2003) — the continuity hypothesis,
  relevant to the dream arriving at accumulated strain rather than at a single fear.
