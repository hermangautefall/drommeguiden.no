## What does it mean to dream of murky water?

You stand at the edge and look down, and there is nothing to see. The water is
brown, clouded or covered with something. You do not know how deep it is, and you do
not know what is down there.

Water in dreams is often tied to feelings. Murky water adds something else: the loss
of visibility. It is not the water that is the problem in this dream — it is that
you cannot see through it.

## The visibility that disappeared

### Not knowing what is down there

Clear water lets you judge before you go in. Cloudy water does not. One reading is
that the dream concerns a situation you cannot get an overview of, however hard you
strain to see.

### When it was clear before

Many remember the water being clean earlier in the dream, or clouding over while they
watched. That transition is usually more important than the state itself, and it is
often described in periods when something manageable has become complicated.

### What lies at the bottom

Water you cannot see through is an old image of the unconscious. Not because
something is wrong down there, but because there is something you have not yet seen.
The dream rarely says what it is.

## What you did with the water

**If you went in anyway:**
Tends to point to a decision you made without full information. Many recognise it
from a specific situation.

**If you turned back:**
Need not be read as cowardice. Declining to enter unclear water is a reasonable
judgement, and the dream rarely condemns it.

**If you tried to see down and could not:**
The most common variant. The effort to see is often the whole dream, and it says more
than whatever might have been there.

**If the water was in a house or a swimming pool:**
Dirty water somewhere that should be clean carries extra weight. Sits close to dreams
of [flooding](/en/dreams/flood/), where water is where it does not belong.

**If you drank it:**
A rarer and more unpleasant variant. Often described in periods when someone has
accepted something they had doubts about.

## When the dream comes in unclear periods

The dream tends to arrive when something is missing information: a diagnosis you are
waiting for, a decision others are making, a relationship where you do not know where
you stand. The absence of visibility is a precise translation of that.

The dream sits close to, but differs from, dreaming of [water](/en/dreams/water/) in
general, where the water's form and your distance from it are the theme. It also
differs from [drowning](/en/dreams/drowning/) — there you are in the water, here you
are usually standing at the edge.

## Sources and further reading

- Carl Gustav Jung, *Man and His Symbols* (1964) — on water as an image of the
  unconscious, relevant to why it is the visibility and not the water that carries the
  meaning.
- G. William Domhoff, *The Scientific Study of Dreams* (2003) — the continuity
  hypothesis, relevant to the dream arriving in periods without an overview.
- Calvin Hall and Robert Van de Castle, *The Content Analysis of Dreams* (1966) —
  normative data, relevant because it shows how much space water occupies among dream
  settings.
